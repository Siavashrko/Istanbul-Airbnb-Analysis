Istanbul Airbnb Market Analysis

A portfolio data project analyzing pricing, occupancy, and revenue patterns in Istanbul Airbnb listings.

Project Goal

Find which neighborhoods and listing features are associated with higher occupancy and stronger revenue performance.

Key Factors

Neighborhood

Location concentration and demand distribution across districts.

Room Type

Entire home vs private room performance difference.

Host Quality

Superhost status impact on listing activity.

Price Positioning

Price level relationship with occupancy and revenue.

Top Findings

Market Concentration

Beyoglu leads active listing concentration and performance.

Active vs Inactive Split

A large share of listings show zero estimated occupancy.

Listing Type Effect

Entire homes are much more common among active listings.

Superhost Effect

Superhost share is significantly higher in active listings.
