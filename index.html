Istanbul Airbnb Market Analysis (2026)

A clean project page with the full analysis structure.

Introduction

Istanbul has thousands of Airbnb listings, but availability does not guarantee performance. This project reviews the market to understand which listings are active, which are inactive, and what separates both groups.

Problem Statement

A large share of listings showed zero estimated occupancy over the previous 365 days. The core question is why some listings attract bookings while many others remain inactive.

Goal

Identify success signals in Istanbul Airbnb listings by measuring occupancy activity, revenue potential, and price positioning by neighborhood and listing profile.

Data Summary

  • Source: Inside Airbnb
  • Snapshot: 30 June 2026
  • Main file: listings.csv.gz
  • Supporting files: calendar.csv.gz, reviews.csv.gz, neighbourhoods.csv, neighbourhoods.geojson
  • Initial listings: 26,631
  • Cleaned sample: 23,467

Data Structure

The analysis is organized around the listing-level grain in listings.csv.gz. Calendar and reviews files are supporting datasets and should be aggregated before joining to listings.

Data Headers

  • id
  • neighbourhood_cleansed
  • room_type, property_type
  • price, estimated_occupancy_l365d, estimated_revenue_l365d
  • accommodates, bedrooms, beds, bathrooms
  • host_is_superhost, number_of_reviews, review_scores_rating

Header Relationships

  • id is the unique listing key.
  • price and estimated_occupancy_l365d jointly influence estimated_revenue_l365d.
  • room_type, property_type, and capacity explain listing positioning.
  • host_is_superhost and review metrics capture trust and service quality.
  • neighbourhood_cleansed captures geographic demand effects.

Data Review

Missing values were found in beds, bathrooms, and review_scores_rating. Extreme price outliers were also detected and removed to keep averages meaningful.

EDA

The exploratory analysis compared active and inactive listings, neighborhood performance, room type distribution, and superhost impact.

Report

Findings

  • Market concentration is strongest in Beyoglu.
  • Active listings are mostly entire homes/apartments.
  • Superhost share is much higher in active listings.
  • Active listings have a much higher median price than inactive listings.

Conclusion

The Istanbul Airbnb market is split between active and inactive listings. Location, room type, host trust, and pricing all shape performance.
